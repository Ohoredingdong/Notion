## What has and has not improved
- Guard is a **future issuer eligibility safety improvement under research**, reducing foreseeable unscorable KR auction-spanning 30-minute targets if later approved. This will *increase no-call* in this market/time segment, **not** retroactively convert unscored cases into predictions, successes, losses or training. Rejecting difficult targets must be counted in coverage and cannot manufacture model precision improvement.
- It does not fix 2026-10-30 14:45–15:15 Yahoo truncation, does not provide authenticated official KRX/NXT-specific 5m bars or quotes, and does not validate actual execution cost or alert reception. US and KR remain separate. Future sources still need session-scoped publication/execution verification and an untouched forward interval.
- Tests/independent audit success are engineering evidence only. Branch remains **unmerged** pending all production-level regression/UI/public release checks and preapproved review. Existing forecast pipeline, model variants including `movement_then_direction`, 120 targets/20 session criterion, 12 strict-green tests, and the separate stock data error monitoring automation unchanged.
- Trial budget used: **1 RED + 1 GREEN**. No unlimited tuning and no reuse of observed missing outcomes for model promotion.
