## Single pre-fixed hypothesis and one fault injection
The independent auditor verifies a scored outcome's `id`, `symbol`, `mode`, target, actual label, price arithmetic and copied public time against its first-issued forecast. It does not independently compare an outcome's explicitly stored `issued_at` with that forecast's frozen `issued_at`. This could silently accept a changed or retrospectively advanced outcome issuance timestamp while leaving all arithmetic and publication checks green. It is a potential **lineage-integrity** weakness only, *not* observed corruption of live records, proof of a retroactive production issuance or evidence of market timing usefulness.

One RED synthetic negative control will create an otherwise valid forecast, matching observed outcome and canonical publication proof, then change only the outcome's `issued_at` (preserving all other fields) while leaving its matching frozen forecast unchanged. Expect auditor's `integrity_errors` with explicit reason `outcome_issued_at_mismatch`. Confirm the legitimate synthetic control passes and the tampered case fails the intended control before implementation. Keep the change scoped to observed outcomes that expressly contain `issued_at`: absence in older synthetic historical fixtures is not retroactively backfilled. Timestamp offsets representing the same UTC instant should remain equal; timezone-naive bad timestamps should be rejected when explicitly supplied. No new model or threshold search is authorized; test budget is exactly one hypothesis, plus any documented regression corrections.
