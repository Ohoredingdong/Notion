# Investment entry plan — entry-plan-1.0

## Scope
This is a fail-closed next-session research plan, not a real-time feed, automated execution, return probability, or validated trading strategy. It separates research ranking, fundamental diligence, timing conditions, and post-decision observations. Existing strict-green-1.0 requirements are not relaxed. OpenDART authentication is left unchanged.

## Gates
The raw source must be at most 18 hours old and share its exact timestamp and SHA-256 with cases/depth and its timestamp with the review. Missing actual price-collection time cannot be replaced by a newly written JSON timestamp. An unfinished regular-session daily bar cannot supply an end-of-day entry plan.

A candidate must have both embedded and standalone boolean green approval, exactly the 12 distinct required checks passing, a valid review interval, matching price/session/forecast EPS, and a fresh re-evaluation of the original strict validator. Source document records and fundamental fingerprints must still agree; no approval records are generated by this feature.

Initial, uncalibrated timing policy: price above the 20- and 50-session averages, 20-session extension between 0% and 5%, RSI between 40 and 70, adequate observed turnover, no unresolved split, and no forecast EPS revision below -3%. These are explicit hypotheses for forward validation, not demonstrated optimal settings. The next-session review ceiling is the minimum of the independent valuation reference, the frozen price plus 2%, and the 20-session average plus 5%. No ceiling is published before the mandatory data/diligence checks pass. A fresh quote and disclosure check is still required next session. The invalidation reference is not a maximum-loss guarantee.

## Honest limitations
No live trade recommendation is enabled. Market scenarios are conditional branches, not forecasts that were made before today's rise. Actual market breadth, sector contribution and investor-flow evidence remain incomplete and are displayed as such. Regular-session close checks are conservative; no next-session holiday calendar is invented. Provider history remains unofficial single-source Yahoo data.

## Audit trail and observations
`stock-entry-ledger.jsonl` appends immutable observations for the full covered universe, including rejected candidates. It never backdates a decision. Repeated identical source/decision states are deduplicated. Review changes can create a new observation without overwriting an old one.

`stock-entry-outcomes.json` compares the frozen reference price with 1/5/20 subsequent provider trading observations whose sessions open after the record timestamp. It reports benchmark price return and excess return, and labels a 30-basis-point round-trip cost subtraction as an assumption. It does not pretend a limit order was filled, include dividends, or call these observations a validated strategy. Future bars, changed baseline prices, splits, missing benchmark data and collection failures do not become successful outcomes. Outcome sample sufficiency, out-of-sample validation and market-regime robustness must be assessed before enabling a live recommendation policy.

## Verification

The workflow separately requests a Pages build and verifies the public JavaScript hash and source snapshot. Repository publication alone is not deployment verification.
